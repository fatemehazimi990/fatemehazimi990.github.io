### 📄 Citation
**Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models**  
Jonathan Ho, Ajay Jain, Pieter Abbeel (NeurIPS 2020).  
[Paper link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239)

### 🧠 Core Idea
DDPM models data generation as a learned reversal of a noise diffusion process.

### 🧪 Key Insight
- Forward process: Adds Gaussian noise
- Reverse process: Trained denoiser neural net

### 🔍 Takeaways
- Foundation for modern diffusion models

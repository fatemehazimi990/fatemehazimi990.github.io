I come from a small town in Iran called [Bardaskan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bardaskan). I earned my bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering from Ferdowsi University of Mashhad and later moved to Germany to pursue a master's degree in Telecommunication and Information Technology at [RWTH Aachen](https://www.rwth-aachen.de/).

I recently completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at [RPTU Kaiserslautern-Landau](https://rptu.de/) under the supervision of [Professor Andreas Dengel](https://www.dfki.uni-kl.de/~dengel/). My thesis, titled "Image and Video Analysis in Challenging Scenarios", focused on advancing deep learning and computer vision techniques in complex environments.

Over the past three years, I have worked in [Algolux]() and [Torc Robotics]() on the perception stack of autonomous driving systems, collaborating closely with [Professor Felix Heide](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/~fheide/), Dr. Fahim Mannan, and Dr. Omid Hosseini Jafari.

I have recently relocated to the beautiful city of Vienna, where I’m joining [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) as a Senior Applied Scientist, focusing on Generative AI.

Outside of work, I enjoy reading, learning new things, and going on long walks.

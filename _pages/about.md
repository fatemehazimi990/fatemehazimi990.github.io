I come from a small town in Iran, called [Bardaskan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bardaskan). I did my bachelor's in Electrical engineering at Ferdowsi University of Mashhad. Afterward, I moved to Germany to pursue my master's studies at [RWTH Aachen](https://www.rwth-aachen.de/) in Telecommunication and Information Technology. 
Currently, I am working as a Ph.D. student at [TU Kaiserslautern](https://www.uni-kl.de/en/) supervised by [professor Andreas Dengel](https://www.dfki.uni-kl.de/~dengel/indexEng.php). My current research focuses on Computer Vision and Deep Learning.
